What To Do If Your Car Breaks Down on the Highway in Dallas (Step-by-Step Guide)

A step-by-step safety guide for breakdowns on busy Dallas–Fort Worth highways.

Move to safety if you can

If the vehicle can still move, carefully get as far from active traffic as possible and turn on the hazard lights. If moving the vehicle is unsafe or impossible, prioritize your own safety and contact emergency services when necessary.

Stay visible

Hazard lights and reflective warning devices can help approaching drivers see a disabled vehicle. Avoid standing in active lanes or working beside fast-moving traffic.

Call for professional assistance

Give dispatch your direction of travel, highway or road name, nearest exit or landmark, vehicle description and the problem you are experiencing. Accurate location details can reduce response delays.
