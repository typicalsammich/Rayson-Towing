What to Tell Dispatch for a Medium-Duty Tow in DFW

Home / Blog / Medium-Duty Towing
Sean Paulsen • 09/13/2026

Medium-duty towing calls are easier to route when dispatch knows exactly what vehicle is disabled and what happened. Box trucks, delivery vehicles, work vans and similar commercial vehicles can require different equipment than a passenger car.

Information to have ready

  • Exact location and safest access point
  • Vehicle year, make, model and body type
  • Approximate weight or GVWR if known
  • Whether wheels roll and steer
  • Collision damage or mechanical failure
  • Destination and approximate tow distance

Photos can also help when the vehicle is in an unusual position. Giving dispatch accurate details up front helps the towing company determine appropriate equipment and avoid unnecessary delays.
