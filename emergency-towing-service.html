Emergency Towing in Cedar Hill & DFW

Emergency towing is for disabled, collision-damaged or unsafe-to-drive vehicles that need prompt transport. When calling, share your exact location, vehicle type, whether the vehicle rolls and steers, and your destination.

Emergency situations we handle

  • Breakdowns and no-start vehicles
  • Accident and collision towing
  • Vehicles blocking a driveway or unsafe location
  • Winch-out and recovery situations
  • After-hours towing throughout the service area
