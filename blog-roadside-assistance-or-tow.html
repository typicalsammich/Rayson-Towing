Roadside Assistance or Towing? How to Know What to Request

Home / Blog / Roadside Assistance
Sean Paulsen • 09/12/2026

Some problems may be resolved at the vehicle, while others require transport. Explaining the symptoms rather than guessing the service can help dispatch determine the next step.

Common roadside situations

  • Dead battery or no-start that may need a jump
  • Flat tire where a usable spare is available
  • Vehicle lockout
  • Vehicle stuck in mud, grass or a ditch
  • Mechanical failure or collision that leaves the vehicle unsafe to drive

If the vehicle cannot be safely driven after roadside help, towing may still be necessary. Tell dispatch what happened and whether the vehicle rolls, steers and brakes normally.
